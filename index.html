The Unscripted Table

A dinner series, by invitation

Around ten guests at one table, in a place disclosed only to those invited. There is no agenda and there are no speakers. The evening runs on conversation, and on the stories people tell when nothing is being presented.

Each guest is chosen individually, from business, finance, science, technology, diplomacy and the arts, with a particular place at the table for women who lead in their fields and for the generation coming next.

A small number of partners support each season. Packages are shaped with us, and a confidential sponsor brief is available on request.

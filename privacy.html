Privacy notice

Harrington Verve Ltd is the controller of the personal data you send through this website.

What we collect

Only what you type into the introduction form: your name, email address, area of interest and, if you choose, a message. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies on this site, and we set no cookies at all.

Why

To reply to your request. Our lawful basis is your request to be contacted (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)) and our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries.

Where it goes

The form is delivered to our inbox through our email provider. The website itself keeps no copy. Enquiry emails are kept for up to 24 months unless a working relationship follows.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, by writing to enquiries@harringtonverve.com. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).
